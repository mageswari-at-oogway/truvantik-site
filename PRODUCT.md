## Platform

## Product purpose

Truvantik is the AI consulting brand of Prabhaavi Solutions. This marketing site explains its services, approach, industries and work, and directs visitors to a discovery conversation.

## Users

Working inference from existing site copy: business and technology leaders evaluating an implementation partner, including teams exploring AI, ready to build, or trying to move a stalled pilot into production. More specific audience prioritization remains open.

## Positioning

The supplied site positions the team as engineering-led, with one partner across Assess, Build and Deploy. Preserve the existing factual copy and qualifications.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Existing Astro site, static output, CSS and small browser scripts.
- Preserve existing routes and contact flow. The contact form has client-side validation but no delivery endpoint; do not imply successful delivery.
- Tapioca Health is a separate product brand, built with 3BP Labs. Representative examples must remain identified as such.
- Do not invent clients, results, certifications or timelines.

## Brand commitments

The user considers the logo shape settled. Preserve its geometry and existing local edits; its approved colors are saffron, soft white and green, echoed in the dark page. The user prefers the earlier dark editorial layouts and animated graphics over the later light/static redesign. Refine that earlier direction, with coordinated palette families and clearer animation. Maintain a canonical `design.md` for consistent future work.

## Evidence on hand

Existing page copy in `src/pages/`, the project README and the website build brief. Quantitative claims and legal placeholders still need the owner's launch review, as recorded in the README.

## Product principles

- Make the offer and next step understandable immediately.
- Show the relationship between assessment, implementation and adoption.
- Keep qualifications alongside the claims they constrain.
- Make navigation and reading usable without motion or JavaScript.
